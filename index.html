Meet Tyra

Licensed Massage Therapist & Wellness Specialist

Hi, I'm Tyra — a licensed massage therapist with a deep passion for helping people feel their very best. I founded Tyra Place because I believe everyone deserves access to high-quality, personalized therapeutic care in a space that feels safe, calm, and welcoming.

My approach blends proven therapeutic techniques with genuine client-centered care. Every session is tailored to your body, your goals, and how you're feeling that day — because no two people, and no two sessions, are ever the same.

Whether you're recovering from muscle tension, managing chronic stress, or simply craving a moment of deep rest, I'm here to support your wellness journey with professionalism, warmth, and expertise.
